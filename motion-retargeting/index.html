Honest scope — what this is NOT

Stages 1–2 are kinematics-only: no dynamics, no torque limits, no contact reasoning — the output is a kinematically feasible retargeted trajectory, and where the robot physically cannot reach the human pose, the residual is reported as-is. Whether those trajectories are dynamically executable is answered separately, under physics: see retargeting + physics.

Read the numbers carefully. The two RMSE columns are not directly comparable DOCUMENTED: Stage 1 IK optimises exactly the position error these metrics measure, while GMR is rotation-weighted against its own scaled targets — its advantage here is visual (natural arm motion, grounded legs, plausible whole-body poses). Attribution: GMR is Retargeting Matters: General Motion Retargeting for Humanoid Motion Tracking (Araújo · Ze · Xu · Wu · Liu, Stanford University, ICRA 2026, MIT licence) — external open-source work, not a group algorithm.
