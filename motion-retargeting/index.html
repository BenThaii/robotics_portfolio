Honest scope — what this is NOT

Stages 1–2 are kinematics-only: no dynamics, no torque limits, no contact reasoning — the output is a kinematically feasible retargeted trajectory, and where the robot physically cannot reach the human pose, the residual is reported as-is. Whether those trajectories are dynamically executable is answered separately, under physics: see retargeting + physics.

Read the numbers carefully. The two RMSE columns are not directly comparable Documented: Stage 1 IK optimises exactly the position error these metrics measure, while GMR is rotation-weighted against its own scaled targets — its advantage here is visual on most clips (natural arm motion, grounded legs, plausible whole-body poses), with the box clip a documented exception below. Attribution: GMR is Retargeting Matters: General Motion Retargeting for Humanoid Motion Tracking (Araújo · Ze · Xu · Wu · Liu, Stanford University, ICRA 2026, MIT licence) — external open-source work, used unchanged; the IK pipeline is this project's own.

Box is the weak spot. Its GMR retarget sits 27.7 cm off at the wrists and 26.8 cm at the elbows, with the arms hanging and spreading instead of holding a boxing guard — 18.9 cm overall against the own-IK clip's 1.7 cm. The 30k RL policy on the imitation-learning page tracks that reference faithfully; the limitation is the reference, not the policy.
