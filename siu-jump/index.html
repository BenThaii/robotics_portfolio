Trajectory generation: three stitched pieces

The robot had no motion capture of a running approach into a jump — the reference had to be built. Three sources, one stitched clip, and machine-checkable gates.

01

The jump — retargeted human motion

A human SIU-celebration clip (AMASS, via the ASAP repo) retargeted to the 22-joint Mini Pi Plus with GMR, plus a stance-floor adjustment so the takeoff foot meets the ground where the robot's foot actually is.

02

The run-up — generated, not captured

The celebration clip has no run-in, so the 2.0 s approach was generated from a free-base AMP rollout in MuJoCo and retargeted onto the robot: 1.211 m of travel at a 1.374 m/s peak, first footfalls 0.10 s and 0.06 s in.

03

The bridge — braking into the crouch

A 0.38 s transition stitches run speed into the crouch takeoff, optimized so the join never penetrates the floor — worst contact 3.6 mm deep across the bridge.

04

The gates — a phase protocol

The reference ships with measurable pass conditions: ≥0.75 m approach at ≥0.7 m/s by frame 100, jump judged in a 2.54–4.09 s window, airborne turn ≥35°, stable finish. Evaluator and training enforce the same gates.

The learning ledger

Warm-start chaining: a policy first trained on the in-place jump, then continued on the combined motion — 900 PPO iterations, 1536 environments, reward 53 → 68. Every stage reported with its number.

The recurring early failure mode is visible in the ledger: not falling — under-running. Braking too early still lands upright, which is why the pass gate is the approach distance, not survival.

Exported artifact

05

ONNX

340 KB graph exported from the shipped checkpoint. Runtime parity against PyTorch: max action difference 2.1e-6 across a full 16-trial evaluation, which itself passes 16/16 on seed 72 through the same phase gates.

06

What is measured vs not

All numbers above are observed in free-base MuJoCo at default conditions (friction 0.6). Reduced-friction and push robustness are unmeasured for this policy, and nothing here has run on hardware.

Constraints: simulation only — no hardware deployment · one policy per motion, trained by warm-start chaining from the standing-SIU checkpoint · robustness beyond default contact conditions not yet characterized.
